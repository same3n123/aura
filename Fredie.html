AI mode

Check the direction words against your arm: type "b +20" in the command box and see which way it turns, then fix the base line if needed. Same for wrist up/down.

With AI on, sentences the app cannot match itself are sent to Claude with this guide, the arm's current angles, your poses and your shortcuts. It replies with moves and a spoken line. Chat that is not about moving just gets a reply.

Tasks

A task is something you say and a list of steps it does. Build steps with the buttons - no codes needed. Say two task names in one sentence to run them one after the other.

Movement phrases

Natural things you say and the single joint move each one means. "extend forward a little" = the phrase plus an amount word. Edit, add, or remove rows.

You sayJointDirection°
a little =° for
a lot =° for
nothing said =°

A spoken number always wins: "extend forward 45" moves 45.

Joint words

For each joint, the words that mean a higher angle (+) and the words that mean a lower angle (−). Comma-separated; add your own. "shoulder up" moves 30° in whichever column "up" is in. A joint with no direction word uses the first + word.

Name and voice

On-device downloads a 40 MB speech model the first time (about a minute), then keeps it. It knows your shortcut phrases and pose names, so commands are caught more reliably. With AI mode on it listens for free speech instead.

Noise filtering

Words it hears but rejects show faintly with a "?" so you can see what the noise sounds like to it. Raise the confidence if it keeps reacting to background sound; lower it if it ignores you.

Backup

Everything - tasks, phrases, face, name, joint words - in one file. The API key is not included unless you tick the box.
